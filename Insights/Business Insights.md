# Business Insights

## Executive Overview

- Total actual power generation reached 66.08M while theoretical generation was 75.40M.
- Operational efficiency remained at 87.64%.
- Total operational power loss reached 9.32M.
- Optimal wind conditions generated the highest power output.

---

## Power Curve Analysis

- Actual turbine output deviated from theoretical power curves during underperforming operational states.
- Medium and high wind speed ranges contributed the highest operational output.
- Underperforming records were concentrated in specific wind speed ranges.
- High wind operational conditions contributed significantly to power loss.

---

## Underperformance Analysis

- Underperformance counts peaked during January and December.
- Operational losses reduced significantly during August and October.
- Hourly underperformance patterns showed increased operational issues during specific hours.
- Root cause analysis identified wind category and performance status as major contributors to operational loss.

---

## Time-Based Performance

- Hourly efficiency varied across operational periods.
- Peak efficiency was observed during March, September, October, and November.
- Evening operational windows generated higher power output.
- Monthly efficiency trends indicated operational variability across the year.

---

## Operational Recommendations

- Improve operational monitoring during high wind conditions.
- Investigate recurring underperformance periods.
- Monitor turbines operating below theoretical expectations.
- Optimize turbine performance during low-efficiency operational windows.
- Implement predictive maintenance and anomaly detection systems.
